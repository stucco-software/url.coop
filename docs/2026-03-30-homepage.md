# Homepage

url.coop needs a landing page explaining what it is, with a CTA that sends user to id.stucco.software/register

## Overview

url.coop is a link-sharing service built on decentralized identity. Users claim a webID (e.g. `yourname.login.stucco.software`) and get a personal profile page displaying their links, bio, and social handles  (e.g. `url.coop/yourname`). Profile data is stored on a Solid pod via Stucco Software, so users own their data.

The homepage is the first thing a visitor sees at `url.coop` before they've claimed a handle. It needs to quickly communicate what the service is and get people to sign up.

## Goals

- Explain what url.coop does in plain language
- Show why it's different (you own your data, decentralized identity)
- Drive sign-ups via a clear CTA to `https://id.stucco.software/account/create`
- Establish the visual identity and tone of the project

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero

- Large title: **url.coop** (using `--sans-stack` / Degular)
- Tagline: **Links for everyone.**
- One-sentence explanation: A personal link page you actually own. Built on open, decentralized identity.
- Primary CTA button: **Claim your page** → `https://id.stucco.software/register`

### 2. How It Works

Three-step breakdown, kept short:

1. **Sign up** — Create a free account with Stucco ID, our identity provider.
2. **Set up your profile** — Add your name, bio, and links — social media, email, websites, whatever you want.
3. **Share your URL** — Get a page at `url.coop/yourname` that you can share anywhere.

### 3. Why url.coop

A few short points that differentiate the service:

- **You own your data** — Your profile lives on Solid ID, a community run identity and storage provider. No platform lock-in, no ads, no tracking.
- **Open standards** — Built on Solid and JSON-LD. Your identity is portable and interoperable.
- **Simple and focused** — One page, your links, your way. No algorithmic feeds or engagement tricks.

## Design Notes

- Use the existing design system: `var.css` tokens, font stacks, rhythm scale
- The hero should feel spacious — generous whitespace, large type (`--txt-3` or `--txt-4` for the title)
- CTA button should stand out — consider using `--red` or `--green` as the button background against `--white` or `--light-gray`
- The "How It Works" section can use the `--mono-stack` (Departure) for step numbers to add typographic variety
- Keep body text in `--sans-stack` (Degular) at `--txt-0`
- The page should feel calm and confident, not busy — match the understated quality of the profile card design
- Responsive: should work well on mobile without special breakpoints (fluid type and spacing already handle this)
- The link hover style (pink highlight) from `global.css` carries over naturally

## Copy Tone

- Direct and unpretentious
- Technical where it matters but don't lead with jargon
- Short sentences, short sections
- Assume the visitor might not know what Solid or decentralized identity means — explain by benefit first, name second. 
- Focus on benefit, not technology

## Implementation Notes

- This is the `src/routes/+page.svelte` file
- No server-side data loading needed — this is a static page
- Keep the component self-contained with scoped `<style>` like the profile page does
- Use existing utility classes from `global.css` where they fit (`.container`, `.text`, font classes)
- The CTA links to an external URL (`https://id.stucco.software/register`) — opens in a new tab or same tab, up to implementation preference
