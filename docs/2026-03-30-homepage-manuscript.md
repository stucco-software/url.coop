## Why url.coop

`[Section heading]`

### Why url.coop

**Your data, your rules**
Your profile lives on Stucco ID, a community-run identity provider. No ads. No tracking. No platform lock-in. You can move your data whenever you want.

**Built on open standards**
url.coop uses Solid and linked data under the hood. Your identity is portable — it works across any service that speaks the same protocols.

**Just your links**
One page. No algorithmic feed, no engagement tricks, no noise. A clean page with your name and your links.
