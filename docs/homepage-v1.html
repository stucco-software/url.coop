How it works

  1. Sign up Create a free account with Stucco ID. It takes a minute.
  2. Add your links Your name, a short bio, and links to wherever you are online. Social profiles, email, websites — whatever matters to you.
  3. Share your URL You get a page at url.coop/yourname. Put it in your bio, send it in a message, print it on a card.

Why url.coop

  • Your data, your rules Your profile lives on Stucco ID, a community-run identity provider. No ads. No tracking. No platform lock-in. You can move your data whenever you want.
  • Built on open standards url.coop uses Solid and linked data under the hood. Your identity is portable — it works across any service that speaks the same protocols.
  • Just your links One page. No algorithmic feed, no engagement tricks, no noise. A clean page with your name and your links.
